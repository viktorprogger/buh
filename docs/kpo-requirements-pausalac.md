# KPO Book Requirements for Serbian Flat-Rate Entrepreneurs (Предузетник-паушалац)

## Legal Basis

The KPO book is regulated by:
- **Правилник о пословним књигама и исказивању финансијског резултата по систему простог књиговодства** (Regulation on Business Books and Financial Results under the Simple Bookkeeping System), issued by the Tax Administration (Пореска управа)
- **Закон о порезу на доходак грађана** (Personal Income Tax Law), which governs flat-rate taxation of entrepreneurs

## What Is the KPO Book

**KPO** = *Књига о оствареном промету паушално опорезованих обвезника*  
(Business Book on Realized Turnover of Flat-Rate Taxed Taxpayers)

It is the **only mandatory business record** a паушалац must maintain. Unlike regular entrepreneurs, паушалци do not keep double-entry bookkeeping (КЕП/КПИ books, balance sheets, income statements). The KPO is a simple chronological income ledger.

## Who Must Keep It

Every **предузетник-паушалац** (entrepreneur taxed on a lump-sum basis) is legally obligated to maintain a KPO book. This applies regardless of activity type or turnover amount, as long as the entrepreneur holds flat-rate taxpayer status.

## Income Limit for Паушалац Status

| Threshold | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| Up to **6,000,000 RSD** per calendar year | Eligible to remain a паушалац |
| **6,000,001 – 8,000,000 RSD** | Must switch to regular bookkeeping (КЕП/КПИ); not subject to VAT |
| Over **8,000,000 RSD** (in any trailing 12 months) | Must register for VAT; flat-rate status is lost |

The KPO book is the primary tool for tracking whether the 6,000,000 RSD annual limit has been breached.

## KPO Book Heading

The heading of each KPO book must identify the entrepreneur and the tax year. The official form requires the following fields at the top:

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **PIB** | Tax identification number (poreski identifikacioni broj) |
| **Obveznik** | Full name and surname of the entrepreneur |
| **Firma-radnje** | Business/trade name |
| **Sedište** | Business seat (address) |
| **Šifra poreskog obveznika** | Taxpayer code assigned by the Tax Administration |
| **Šifra delatnosti** | Activity classification code (industry code) |

The book is maintained per calendar year — a new book is opened on 1 January of each year, so the year is implicit from the book itself (serial numbers restart from 1 each January).

### Certification Before First Entry

The rules differ depending on the format:

- **Paper form**: Must be certified (overena) by the competent tax authority before the first entry is made. No explicit financial penalty is prescribed for skipping this, but an uncertified paper book may be challenged during a tax inspection.
- **Electronic form**: No prior certification is required or possible (the tax authority cannot stamp a file). Keeping the book electronically is therefore not blocked by this requirement.

### Closing / Signing at Year-End

- **Paper form**: Closed with an official stamp and signature at year-end.
- **Electronic form**: Should be **printed, reviewed, and signed** at year-end before archiving. The regulation requires the book to be printable or displayable on screen on demand, but practical guidance from accounting sources consistently calls for a signed printed copy as the final record.

## KPO Book Structure (Columns)

The official form has exactly **5 columns**:

| Column | Content |
|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Serial number** — sequential from 1, restarted each calendar year |
| 2 | **Date and description** — date of the transaction and a brief description (invoice number, client name, nature of service/product) |
| 3 | **Revenue from sale of products** (goods) |
| 4 | **Revenue from services performed** |
| 5 | **Total revenue** (sum of columns 3 and 4) |

At year-end, all columns are summed and a closing total is shown before the book is closed for that year.

## What Is Entered

- Every **issued invoice**, in chronological order by issue date
- Both **cash and non-cash** transactions
- **Domestic and foreign currency** invoices (foreign currency amounts converted to RSD at the exchange rate on the date of invoicing)

## What Is NOT Entered

- **Advance payments received** before the service is performed or goods delivered — entered only when the final invoice is issued
- **Expenses and costs** — паушалци do not record expenditures in the KPO
- **Loans, capital contributions**, or other non-revenue receipts
- **Refunds or cancellations** of previously issued invoices (handled via corrective entries or storno, but the original entry is not deleted)

## When to Enter

Entries are made **by invoice issue date**, not by payment receipt date. If an invoice is issued in December but paid in January of the following year, it is entered in December's records.

## Format

The KPO book may be maintained:
- **In paper form** (printed official form, or a notebook matching the prescribed column structure)
- **Electronically** (Excel, Word, or accounting software) — provided it can be printed or displayed on screen when required by the Tax Administration

A separate KPO book is maintained for **each calendar year**.

## Submission and Reporting

- The KPO book is **not submitted** to the Tax Administration on a regular basis
- It must be **available for inspection** upon Tax Administration request
- The annual total from the KPO book feeds into the **ПPDГ-1Р** (Пореска пријава за утврђивање пореза и доприноса за обавезно социјално осигурање самоопорезивањем на приходе од самосталне делатности) — the annual self-assessment return, if applicable

## Retention Period

The KPO book must be retained for a minimum of **5 years** from the date the book is closed at year-end.

## Obligations Related to Flat-Rate Status

Additional obligations that interact with the KPO:

- **Monthly tax and contribution payments** — due by the **15th of each month**, based on the lump-sum amount set by the Tax Administration via решење (administrative decision); the amount does not vary with actual income
- **Annual request to retain паушалац status** — must be submitted electronically via the **eПорези** portal by **31 October** of the preceding year (e.g., by 31 October 2025 for the 2026 tax year); failure to submit means automatic transition to the regular bookkeeping system
- **No VAT registration** — a паушалац cannot be in the VAT system; registration for VAT triggers immediate loss of flat-rate status

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to maintain or produce the KPO book may result in:
- Tax inspections and estimated income assessments
- Financial penalties under the Law on Tax Procedure and Tax Administration
- Loss of паушалац status
